Methodology

A deliberate, low-drama delivery method

Every engagement follows the same five phases, so you always know what is changing, when it changes, and how we reverse it if it misbehaves.

PHASE 01

Discover

Inventory the estate, map dependencies and surface the constraints that usually derail projects late.

PHASE 02

Design

A target architecture sized to the actual workload and budget, with trade-offs documented, not hidden.

PHASE 03

Pilot

Prove the design on a representative slice and measure it before anything production-critical moves.

PHASE 04

Deliver

Phased cutover with validation gates, agreed success criteria and a rehearsed rollback at each step.

PHASE 05

Hand over

As-built documentation, runbooks and working sessions so your team can operate it unaided.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

How does a typical engagement start?

With a short, no-obligation call to understand the problem. If it looks like a fit, we propose a fixed-scope assessment with a defined deliverable and price. You are free to take that roadmap and implement it yourself, or with any partner you choose.

Do you work alongside our existing MSP or internal team?

Routinely. We are often brought in for a specific capability gap — a migration, a hardening programme, a recurring incident nobody has closed out — and work as an extension of the existing team rather than a replacement for it.

Do you resell licences or hardware?

No. We take no resale margin, referral fee or vendor commission of any kind. Our only revenue is the engineering time you engage us for, which is what keeps the architecture advice honest.

How is pricing structured?

Assessments are fixed price against a written scope. Projects are milestone-based with agreed acceptance criteria per milestone. Retainers are a monthly block of hours. We do not bill open-ended time and materials without a cap.

Can you work remotely, and in which time zones?

Delivery is remote-first and we work primarily in IST, with overlap available for EMEA and APAC business hours. Cutover windows and maintenance work are scheduled around your change calendar, including nights and weekends where required.

What happens at the end of an engagement?

You receive the as-built documentation, runbooks and configuration artefacts, plus a handover session with the people who will operate the platform. There is no dependency on us to keep it running, and no proprietary tooling left behind.

Get in touch

Have a migration, an outage, or a platform that has outgrown its design?

Describe the situation and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit, roughly what it should take, and what we would do first. If it is not a fit, we will say so and point you somewhere better.
